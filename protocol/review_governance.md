# Review governance and calibration

This note records the review roles needed to interpret the manuscript's reliability claims without overstating independent recoding.

## Three-author calibration pilot

A heterogeneous 12-study pilot was independently completed by all three authors before the final central-corpus synthesis. The pilot was used to align construct definitions, causal-interpretability grading, resource-equivalence grading, provenance grading, evidence locators, inclusion decisions, and claim-tier decisions.

- Pilot completion: 12/12 studies for each of the three authors.
- Exact three-author agreement: 12/12 for provenance grade, resource-equivalence grade, claim tier, and inclusion decision.
- Causal-interpretability grade: exact agreement for 8/12 studies; all remaining differences were confined to one adjacent ordinal category.
- Krippendorff-style ordinal alpha with squared-distance weighting for causal interpretability: approximately 0.70.
- Numeric pilot block totals were used only as calibration aids and are not used as scalar study-quality scores in the final synthesis.

## Central 82-study synthesis

The final central-corpus workflow combined deterministic preprocessing, AI-assisted evidence localization / first-pass semantic coding, and human adjudication. Final eligibility decisions, construct grades, causal interpretations, and manuscript claims remained human decisions.

Machine non-detection was never automatically converted into evidence of absence. Candidate positive rows and headline zero/small-number cells were checked against retained full-text evidence locators. Therefore, a 0/82 result means that the strict pass did not identify an unambiguous case satisfying the prespecified criterion; it is not a universal claim that the construct is absent from the field.

## Targeted post-corpus coauthor sign-off

A later 18-study high-impact sample received targeted scientific review by the two coauthors who did not perform the principal final adjudication. Both returned favorable overall sign-off and requested no amendments. That stage did **not** produce duplicate construct-by-construct rating matrices. It is therefore treated as a scientific stability / sanity check, not as a second inter-rater study, and no additional agreement statistic is claimed from it.

## AI-assistance boundary

AI assistance was used for evidence localization, first-pass semantic coding, structural editing, language refinement, and consistency checking. Deterministic scripts were used where exact reproducibility was required. AI output did not independently determine inclusion/exclusion, resolve ambiguous evidence, assign final scientific interpretation, or authorize manuscript claims.
