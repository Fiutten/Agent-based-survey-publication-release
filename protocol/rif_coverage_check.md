# RIF post-freeze coverage check

This protocol documents a post-freeze applicability check of the Reliable Information Fusion (RIF) reference/evaluation model. It is **not** part of the frozen systematic-review denominators and is **not** presented as predictive validation of RIF.

## Selection and timing

Eight candidate publications were fixed by the archived deterministic selection rule before RIF coding. Seven were technically assessable at full text; one was retained as a technical exclusion. Candidate identities and coded RIF coverage are reported in `data/rif_external_coverage.tsv`.

## Purpose

The check asks whether the RIF dimensions and typed roles can describe reliability-relevant properties in post-freeze systems that were not used to construct the frozen 175/82 evidence counts. It provides evidence about applicability and discriminative coverage only.

It does **not** establish:

- prevalence of RIF dimensions in the literature;
- predictive validity;
- causal benefit from using RIF during system design;
- superiority over alternative evaluation frameworks;
- a preregistered falsification test.

## Coding levels

The public table uses:

- `R`: reported;
- `E`: empirically evaluated;
- `C`: criterion-satisfying;
- `--`: no unambiguous retained evidence at that level;
- `NA`: not assessed because of technical exclusion.

The criterion-satisfying level is construct-specific and deliberately conservative. It should not be interpreted as a generic synonym for numerical measurement.

## Relation to the primary review

None of these records changes the 3,367-record screening universe, 175-publication structured evidence map, or 82-publication central full-text nucleus. Later database updates, when performed before submission, must be reported separately rather than silently changing the frozen denominators.
